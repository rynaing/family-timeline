Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

What we store

Your family's name and the memories you add: titles, dates, stories, the name you type in "Your name", and any photos. Photos are kept in private storage and are only shown through links that expire after an hour.

If you set up a family, we also keep your email address. It's only used to send you sign-in codes so you can manage the family, never for marketing.

Who can see it

Anyone with your family code can see the whole timeline and add to it, so only share the code with family. A friends link shows titles and dates only, never stories, photos or names, and you can turn old friends links off from the Share menu.

On your device

Kintime doesn't use cookies for tracking or ads. Your browser's own storage remembers which family you joined, whether you like dark mode, and the name you add memories under, so you don't have to type them again. If you sign in as a family's owner, it also keeps your sign-in until you sign out. "Switch family" in the menu clears the family from your device.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies or follow you to other sites, and it isn't loaded on friends links. We also count a few actions in our own database, like "a memory was added" or "a share link was copied", with only the action, which family it was, and the time. Never titles, stories, names or photos.

What we don't do

No ads, and we never sell your family's memories or your email, or share them with anyone except the services below that run Kintime for us.

Services we use

Supabase stores the timelines, photos and owner emails (in the United States). GitHub Pages serves the website, jsDelivr serves one of its code libraries, Cloudflare counts visits, and Resend sends sign-in codes. Each of them sees what it needs to do that job, such as your IP address when your browser connects to it.

Early access

Kintime is free while it's in early access. If we add paid plans later, we'll say so in the app before anything changes for your family.

Backups and deleting

Anyone in the family can download a backup of the timeline's text from the menu. A family's owner can remove any memory and its photos. To have a whole family's timeline deleted, the owner can email us.

Early-access terms

Kintime is a small early-access project, provided as is. We work to keep your memories safe, but we can't promise the service will always be available or that nothing will ever be lost, so keep your own copies of photos that matter and download a backup now and then. Only add things you have the right to share, and be thoughtful about other people's private details.
